1. Healthcare – Increase in Patient Waiting Times

Scenario: A hospital notices that patients are spending more time waiting to consult doctors in the outpatient department.

Descriptive Analysis – What happened?
Patients are waiting longer than usual, and the hospital is receiving more complaints about delays.

Diagnostic Analysis – Why did it happen?
The hospital may be receiving more patients than expected, some doctors may be unavailable, and appointments may not be scheduled efficiently.

Predictive Analysis – What might happen next?
Waiting times could increase further, patients may cancel appointments, and the overall quality of patient care could be affected.

Prescriptive Analysis – What should we do?
The hospital should improve its appointment system, use past patient data to predict busy periods, assign staff more effectively, and inform patients about expected waiting times.


2. Banking – Increase in Failed Digital Transactions

Scenario: A bank notices a sudden increase in failed UPI transactions, leaving customers frustrated.

Descriptive Analysis – What happened?
Many customers are experiencing failed payments, and some are facing delays in receiving refunds.

Diagnostic Analysis – Why did it happen?
The issue could be caused by server overload, network problems, payment system downtime, or technical issues between the bank and the payment interface.

Predictive Analysis – What might happen next?
If the issue continues, customer complaints may increase, people may lose confidence in digital payments, and the bank could face a decline in customer satisfaction.

Prescriptive Analysis – What should we do?
The bank should monitor transactions in real time, identify and fix the technical issue, use backup systems where possible, and keep customers informed about transaction failures and refunds.


3. Education – Declining Student Performance

Scenario: A college finds that students' examination results have been getting worse over the past two semesters.

Descriptive Analysis – What happened?
Average marks have dropped, and more students are struggling to pass important subjects.

Diagnostic Analysis – Why did it happen?
Students may have gaps in their basic understanding, attendance may be low, or the teaching methods may not be effective enough. A lack of practical learning could also be contributing to the problem.

Predictive Analysis – What might happen next?
If the situation continues, more students may fail their examinations, lose confidence, or take longer to complete their degrees.

Prescriptive Analysis – What should we do?
The college should identify the subjects in which students struggle most, arrange additional classes, provide individual support where needed, and include more practical activities to help students understand concepts better.


4. Transportation – Frequent Bus Delays

Scenario: A city's public buses are frequently running late during morning peak hours.

Descriptive Analysis – What happened?
Buses are arriving later than scheduled, causing passengers to reach their offices and colleges late.

Diagnostic Analysis – Why did it happen?
Heavy traffic, road construction, unexpected vehicle breakdowns, and unrealistic bus schedules could be responsible for the delays.

Predictive Analysis – What might happen next?
If the delays continue, passengers may become dissatisfied, buses could become more crowded, and some commuters may switch to private vehicles, making traffic even worse.

Prescriptive Analysis – What should we do?
The transport department should use GPS data to identify routes with frequent delays, adjust bus schedules, improve vehicle maintenance, and provide passengers with real-time updates about bus arrival times.


5. Cybersecurity – Suspicious Login Attempts

Scenario: A company notices an unusual increase in failed login attempts across employee accounts.

Descriptive Analysis – What happened?
Several employee accounts are receiving repeated failed login attempts, some of which originate from unfamiliar IP addresses.

Diagnostic Analysis – Why did it happen?
The activity could be caused by automated password attacks, attempts to use stolen credentials, or someone trying to gain unauthorised access to company systems.

Predictive Analysis – What might happen next?
If the activity is malicious and goes unchecked, an attacker could gain access to an employee account, expose sensitive information, or disrupt business operations.

Prescriptive Analysis – What should we do?
The company should investigate login records, enable multi-factor authentication, limit repeated login attempts, block confirmed malicious sources, secure any compromised accounts, and alert the security team.
